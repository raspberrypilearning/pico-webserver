In this project, you will turn a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W into a tiny web server.

You will:

- connect your Pico 2 W to Wi-Fi
- upload a Python program called `main.py`
- create a `www` folder on the Pico
- add an `index.html` file
- open the page from another device on your local network

By the end, your Pico 2 W will serve a web page from its own file system.
